Rules

Your unlocked cards are shuffled and dealt between you and the computer. Each round both top cards meet. Whoever won the last round picks the stat. Higher number takes both cards. On a tie, a shielded card wins; otherwise both cards go to the pot and the next winner takes it. Hold every card to win, or hold the most after 20 rounds.
