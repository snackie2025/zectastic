Before you go

  • Don't talk about what you hold. Take your badge and merch off when you leave the venue.
  • Leave hardware wallets and seed phrases at home. Carry a small spending wallet only.
  • Don't scan unknown QR codes, plug in free USB gadgets, or sign anything on the spot.
  • Post photos and your location after you have left, not while you are there.
  • Check your government's travel advice for the country you are visiting.

We list the city only, never a venue or address, and only events the organiser promotes publicly. Private and invite-only meetups are not listed.

Dates and venues change. Confirm on the official page before you book. Monthly Zcash community calls are listed in the ZecHub newsletter. Missing an event? Tell us on X.
