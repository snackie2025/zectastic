◈ How to read the map

Gold pulses are buys. The teal zone is everything you can't place.

Each buy flies into the price and lights it up. Swaps from other chains into ZEC surface in the shielded zone, because there is no honest place to put them on a map.

◈

Exchange buys

Every buy on Kraken, Coinbase and Binance appears as it happens. Locations are estimated from the currency of each trade: EUR lands in Europe, JPY in Japan, USD across a few US cities.

◈

Swaps into ZEC

Swaps from other chains into ZEC through NEAR Intents show up in the zone. Only chain and size are used, never addresses. They appear a few minutes after they settle, and some go to shielded addresses, some to transparent ones.

◈

Why a hidden zone

Shielded Zcash hides amounts and parties, so there is nothing to place on a map. The zone shows that activity happens, not who or where.
