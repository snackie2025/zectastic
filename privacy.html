◈ Privacy

What Zectastic and Captastic do and don't do with your data

Short version: there's no account, no wallet connection, and nothing to sell. Anything you save, such as a watchlist or a portfolio, stays in your own browser. Most of what you see is fetched live from other people's public feeds, and this page explains exactly which ones, and what they can see.

Last updated October 2026

◈ The short version

  • → Zectastic doesn't have user accounts, doesn't ask for a wallet address, and doesn't run analytics or advertising trackers.
  • → A few things are stored locally in your browser: your cookie choice, and, if you use them, your Captastic settings, watchlist and portfolio and your game progress. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and nothing here is sold or shared.
  • → Live prices, supply, rankings and shielded-pool numbers come from third-party services (mainly CoinGecko, Binance, Kraken and zkp.baby) that your browser talks to directly. Their own privacy practices are outside our control, and linked below.
  • → The ZEC Map connects your browser to several exchanges at once while it is open, to show their public trades.
  • → Playing the radio opens a direct audio connection to DANCE.FM, which can see your IP address for the duration of playback, same as any internet radio.

◈ What we store ourselves

The site keeps a small amount of information in your browser's local storage, on your own device. It is never sent to us, because there is no server of ours to send it to.

  • Whether you've accepted or dismissed the cookie notice, so it doesn't show up on every visit.
  • Your Captastic settings: the currency, time period and layout you last chose, and whether you hid your portfolio amounts.
  • Your Captastic watchlist: the list of projects you marked.
  • Your Captastic portfolio: the projects and amounts you typed in.
  • A copy of the public top 100 list, so Captastic opens quickly and keeps working when the ranking service is busy.
  • Your game progress: the callsign you chose, your best score and level, and whether you have already seen the intro.

You can remove all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Doing so also erases your watchlist and portfolio, since no other copy exists.

That's the entire list. We don't collect names, emails, wallet addresses, transaction history, or device fingerprints, and we don't run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any similar tracker anywhere on the site.

◈ Captastic: top 100, watchlist and portfolio

Captastic shows the top 100 projects by market cap, and its portfolio can hold projects ranked up to 500. The ranking, supply figures and logos come from CoinGecko, and live prices and chart history come from Binance, both fetched directly by your browser.

Your watchlist and portfolio never leave your browser. The projects you mark and the amounts you type in are saved on your own device only. There is no account, and nothing is uploaded.

For projects in the top 500, the portfolio page asks the price services for the same general data whatever you hold, so they can't tell from its requests which projects are in your portfolio or how much you have. The one exception is a token you add yourself by its contract address: its price is requested by that address from DexScreener, which can then see which token your browser asks about, though never how much of it you hold. The watchlist page loads chart history for the projects on your list, so Binance can see which projects' charts your browser asks for, as it would if you looked them up there.

"Save backup file" writes your holdings to a file on your own device, and "Restore backup" reads that file back. The file goes nowhere else. Because nothing is stored online, your watchlist and portfolio don't follow you to another browser or device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

◈ The ZEC Map

While the map is open, your browser connects directly to the exchanges listed above to receive their public trade feeds. The map only receives data; it sends nothing about you beyond what any connection reveals, such as your IP address.

The places where buys appear are estimates based on the currency of each trade, not the location of any person. Swaps shown in the shielded zone use only the chain, token and size from the public swap feed.

◈ The game and its hall of fame

"Encrypt the money" runs entirely in your browser. The callsign you choose, your best score and your progress are kept on your own device and are not sent anywhere. There is no online scoreboard and no database.

The hall of fame shown in the game is a short list we keep by hand. It is filled from scores that players choose to post publicly on X with #EncryptTheMoney. If you never post, you never appear on it.

The share button opens a ready-made post on X only when you press it, and the post includes your callsign and score. Nothing is posted for you, and what you publish there is covered by X's own rules.

◈ The Flippening page

The flippening leaderboard pulls the same CoinGecko market data as the main tracker, plus a broader list of top coins for comparison. Party mode's confetti and screen effects run entirely in your browser and never leave your device. Starting party mode also starts the DANCE.FM stream described above.

◈ No wallets, no addresses

Zectastic never asks you to connect a wallet, paste an address, or sign anything. Nothing on this site can see your ZEC balance, your transaction history, or which pool your funds sit in. The Captastic portfolio only knows the amounts you type in by hand, and keeps them in your browser. If you're ever asked to connect a wallet on a page claiming to be Zectastic, that page isn't us.

◈ Children's privacy

Zectastic isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

◈ Changes to this page

As the site evolves, this page gets updated to match. Significant changes will be reflected in the date at the top. There's no mailing list to notify, since we don't collect emails to notify anyone with.
