Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

The short version: Zectastic doesn't ask you to connect a wallet, doesn't collect your ZEC address, and doesn't sell data to anyone. We store the minimum needed to run a live price tracker and a browser game.

01 — What we collect

DataPurposeStored where
Cookie consent choiceRemember your preference so we don't ask every visitYour browser (localStorage), not our servers
Game leaderboard score + display nameShow the global leaderboardOur server, tied to a random session ID — not your identity
Basic server logsSecurity, abuse prevention, uptime debuggingOur server, IP addresses auto-purged after 30 days

02 — What we don't collect

  • We never request wallet connections, seed phrases, or private keys — Zectastic is a read-only price tracker and has no reason to ever ask for these.
  • We don't track you across other websites.
  • We don't build advertising profiles, and we don't sell or share data with data brokers.
  • No account or email sign-up is required to use the tracker or play the game.

03 — Cookies & local storage

Zectastic uses essential storage only by default: a single entry in your browser recording your consent choice and, optionally, your radio volume. Neither is sent to our servers or any third party.

If we ever add optional, privacy-respecting analytics (no cross-site tracking, no ad targeting), it will stay off unless you switch it on in Cookie Settings. You can change your choice at any time from the footer of any page.

04 — Third-party data sources

Live prices are pulled directly from CoinGecko's public API. The market cap chart's data comes from CoinPaprika (or CoinGecko as a fallback), relayed through a public CORS proxy (allorigins.win) because our static hosting can't make these cross-origin requests directly — that proxy sees the same request your browser would otherwise send straight to CoinPaprika/CoinGecko. Shielded pool and Ironwood migration data is shown via an embedded live dashboard from zkp.baby — since that's an embedded page rather than a data call, your browser communicates with zkp.baby directly and its own privacy practices apply to that part of the page. The radio stream is served by Zeno.fm. None of these requests send any personal data beyond what any standard web request includes (like your IP address, which each service may log per their own policies).

05 — The leaderboard

Scores are validated server-side before posting — we don't trust client-submitted numbers at face value. Display names are filtered for abuse; don't use one that identifies you personally, since leaderboard entries are public by design.

06 — Your choices

  • Clear your cookie consent choice anytime via Cookie Settings in the footer.
  • Clear localStorage manually in your browser settings to remove all locally stored data instantly.
  • Contact us to request removal of a leaderboard entry.

07 — Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and note it on the homepage. We won't quietly expand what we collect.
