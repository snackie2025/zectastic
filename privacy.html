◈ Privacy

What Zectastic does and doesn't do with your data

Short version: there's no account, no wallet connection, and nothing to sell. Most of what you see is fetched live from other people's public feeds, and this page explains exactly which ones, and what they can see.

Last updated August 2026

◈ The short version

  • → Zectastic doesn't have user accounts, doesn't ask for a wallet address, and doesn't run analytics or advertising trackers.
  • → The only thing stored locally is your cookie choice and, if you set one, your radio volume. Nothing here is sold or shared.
  • → Live price, supply, and shielded-pool numbers come from third-party services (CoinGecko and zkp.baby) that your browser talks to directly. Their own privacy practices are outside our control, and linked below.
  • → Playing the radio opens a direct audio connection to DANCE.FM, which can see your IP address for the duration of playback, same as any internet radio.

◈ What we store ourselves

Zectastic stores essential preferences only, using your browser's local storage:

  • Whether you've accepted or dismissed the cookie notice, so it doesn't show up on every visit.
  • Your radio volume, if you change it from the default.

That's the entire list. We don't collect names, emails, wallet addresses, transaction history, or device fingerprints, and we don't run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any similar tracker anywhere on the site.

◈ Third-party feeds this site uses

To keep prices, supply figures, and the shielded pool dashboard live, your browser fetches data directly from the services below. That means each one can see technical details like your IP address and request timing when your browser calls them, the same as visiting their site directly. Zectastic itself never sees or stores that data.

◈ The Flippening page

The flippening leaderboard pulls the same CoinGecko market data as the main tracker, plus a broader list of top coins for comparison. Party mode's confetti and screen effects run entirely in your browser and never leave your device. Starting party mode also starts the DANCE.FM stream described above.

◈ No wallets, no addresses

Zectastic never asks you to connect a wallet, paste an address, or sign anything. Nothing on this site can see your ZEC balance, your transaction history, or which pool your funds sit in. If you're ever asked to connect a wallet on a page claiming to be Zectastic, that page isn't us.

◈ Children's privacy

Zectastic isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

◈ Changes to this page

As the site evolves, this page gets updated to match. Significant changes will be reflected in the date at the top. There's no mailing list to notify, since we don't collect emails to notify anyone with.
